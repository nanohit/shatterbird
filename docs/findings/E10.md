# E10 — Small 3D

**Run:** `cargo run --release --bin e10` (≈20 s) → `results/E10/`.
**Model:** 3D RBSN on Voronoi cells of a 1 m box (RSA, $d_{\min}$ 0.2 m → 109–112 cells, 0.15 m → 246 cells). 6 DOFs per cell (translation, rotation vector), full inertia tensors from the polyhedra. Per facet, 3 translational springs ($k_n=EA/\ell_e$, $k_t=\gamma k_n$ along principal in-plane axes) and 3 rotational: bending $k_nI_{\perp}/A$ about each principal axis, torsion $k_t(I_1+I_2)/A$. These are the exact integrals of a uniformly distributed facet spring. Linearised kinematics (large rotation was tested in 2D, E4). Clamped bottom face, top traction (2.5, 1.0, −10) kPa. Elastic only.

## Results

**Two cells:** free-pair stiffness has exactly **6 zero modes** (rigid body); torsion stiffness of a unit-square facet $=k_n/6$ ($I_p/A$), as expected.

**Valence** (cell-cell faces per cell): interior cells mean 14.7–15.1, min 10–11, **max 18–19**; overall max 19. The context's "14–16 faces" is a mean, and the tail reaches 19 (context §6.4 asked for this).

**Per-cell 6×6 blocks normalised by blockdiag($m/h^2$, $I/h^2$)**, $h=1/480$ s:

| policy | eigenvalue range (all cells) | bound $\max(1+\sum k_{\rm eff}\lVert v\rVert^2)$ | max λ/bound | max block condition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ALM $\rho=m_{\rm eff}/h^2$ | [1.9, 13.2] | 43–49 | 0.34–0.36 | **5.7–6.9** |
| penalty $k_{\rm eff}=k$ | [2.3·10³, 4.1·10⁶] | 7.6·10⁵–1.4·10⁷ | 0.32–0.34 | 107–1 008 |

The bound holds, is loose by ≈3×, and scales with valence × components (6 per facet). Bounded $k_{\rm eff}$ does make the local blocks well conditioned. As E1–E3 showed, local conditioning is not the bottleneck.

**Force convergence** (iterations to 1 % L2 spring-force error; error after 8 / 16 iterations):

| method | quasi-static cold start | first dynamic step, $h=1/480$ |
|---|---|---|
| S1 per-spring $\rho=0.1k$ | 76–80 (0.41–0.59 / 0.27–0.38) | 74–78 |
| S1 shared $\rho=0.1\bar k$ | 121–183 | — |
| S3 penalty | 359–694 | 345–666 |
| S1 $\rho=m_{\rm eff}/h^2$ | — | 23 892–37 041 |

2D E2 for comparison: per-spring 75–100, shared 125–175, S3 225–375.

## Interpretation

- **2D conclusions transfer to 3D.** Same ordering of policies, same order of magnitude of work, and 1 % is not reached in 8–16 sweeps. The ALM-note policy is again worst when $\pi_h\gg1$ (here ≈1 360). Decision-map entry "E10 contradicts 2D": **not triggered**.
- Q4: normalising translation by $m/h^2$ and rotation by $I/h^2$ (full tensor) gives 6×6 block condition numbers ≤ 7 with dynamic ρ. A fixed-point block solve is numerically unproblematic in that regime; the hard part is global.
- Limits: linearised kinematics, no fracture in 3D, one block shape.
